Community Event Portal Help Center

How to Register?

1. Open the Event Registration Form.
2. Enter your details.
3. Select an event type.
4. Click Register.
5. Confirmation will be displayed.

Available Events

  • Community Meetings
  • Sports Events
  • Food Festivals
  • Music Programs
  • Workshops
  • Awareness Campaigns

Location Feature

Use the "Find Nearby Events" button to view your current coordinates.
